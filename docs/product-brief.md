# Product brief

## Mission

Build a purpose-made land sales and receivables platform that always answers:
what land is available, who owns or reserves it, who owes money, in which
currency, and which customer needs follow-up.

## Product principles

1. Inventory and balances are derived from contracts and transactions, never
   manually synchronized fields.
2. Original monetary facts are immutable. Corrections use reversals or explicit
   adjustments so history remains auditable.
3. Every payment preserves its original currency, original amount, conversion
   rate, converted amount, method, and payment type.
4. Contract lifecycle and payment health are separate concepts.
5. Receipts are generated from persisted transactions and use server-assigned,
   sequential identifiers.
6. The interface is responsive by default and usable by field staff on phones.

## Delivery phases

### Phase 1: trustworthy MVP

- Projects and lots with derived availability
- Customers and prospects
- Contracts with price, down payment, term, schedule, and contract currency
- Multi-currency payments and a transactional balance
- Receipt generation for down payments, installments, and full payment
- Server-computed payment health

### Phase 2: operations and control

- Automated WhatsApp, SMS, or email reminders
- Roles, permissions, and audit history
- Cancellation, default, payoff, and automatic lot release workflows
- Contract restructuring with preserved history
- Customer and contract documents
- Fast field search and printing

### Phase 3: growth

- Cash-flow forecasting
- Prospect and sales pipeline
- Project profitability and sales dashboards
- Offline field workflows
- Accounting exports
- Optional interest and late-fee rules
